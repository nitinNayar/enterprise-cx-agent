## 🛒 Ordering & Payment

### What payment methods do you accept?
- All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- Debit cards
- PayPal
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Bookly Gift Cards
- Buy Now, Pay Later options (Affirm, Klarna)

### Is it safe to use my credit card on Bookly?
Yes! We use industry-standard SSL encryption and PCI-DSS compliant payment processing. We never store your complete credit card information on our servers.

### Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
- **Before Shipping:** Yes, contact us immediately. If the order hasn't been processed, we can modify or cancel it.
- **After Shipping:** You'll need to wait for delivery and process a return.
- **Digital Products:** Cannot be canceled once downloaded/accessed.

### Do you charge sales tax?
Sales tax is calculated based on your shipping address and applicable state/local laws. Tax will be shown at checkout before you complete your purchase.

### Can I use multiple discount codes?
Only one promotional code can be used per order. The system will automatically apply the best available discount.

### What if a book is out of stock?
- You can join the waitlist to be notified when it's back in stock
- Check the "Notify Me" button on the product page
- Consider our e-book or audiobook version if available
- We'll suggest similar titles you might enjoy

## 📦 Shipping & Delivery

### How much is shipping?
- **Standard Shipping:** FREE on orders over $35, otherwise $4.99
- **Expedited (2-3 days):** $9.99
- **Express (1-2 days):** $19.99
- **International:** Starting at $14.99

### How long does shipping take?
- **Standard:** 5-7 business days
- **Expedited:** 2-3 business days
- **Express:** 1-2 business days
- **International:** 10-21 business days

### Do you ship internationally?
Yes! We ship to over 100 countries. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination.

### Can I track my order?
Absolutely! You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships. You can also track orders in your account under "My Orders."

### Do you ship to PO Boxes?
Yes, we ship to PO Boxes using USPS for domestic orders. Express and international shipping to PO Boxes may have limitations.

### What if my package is lost or damaged?
Contact us immediately! We'll work with the carrier to investigate and will send a replacement or issue a refund. We're responsible until you receive your order safely.

## 📖 Product Questions

### What's the difference between hardcover and paperback?
- **Hardcover:** Durable hard cover, sewn binding, premium quality, typically more expensive
- **Paperback:** Flexible cover, lighter weight, more affordable, easier to carry
- **Trade Paperback:** Larger paperback with higher quality paper

### What formats are e-books available in?
Our e-books are available in multiple formats:
- EPUB (most e-readers)
- PDF (universal)
- MOBI (Kindle-compatible)
- Auto-delivery to your preferred reading app

### Can I read e-books on multiple devices?
Yes! Purchase once and access on unlimited devices. Download from your Bookly Library on any device.

### What's the difference between e-books and audiobooks?
- **E-books:** Digital text you read on screen
- **Audiobooks:** Audio narration you listen to
- Both are instantly downloadable and accessible in your Bookly Library

### Do you sell used books?
Currently, we only sell new books. However, we have a "Good Deal" section with discounted new books.

### Are signed copies available?
Select titles are available with author signatures. Look for the "Signed Edition" badge on product pages. These are limited quantities and may sell out quickly.

### Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes! Add gift wrapping at checkout for $4.99. Includes decorative wrapping and a custom message card.

## 🔄 Returns & Refunds

### What is your return policy?
Physical books can be returned within 30 days if they're in unread, resellable condition. See our complete [Return Policy](return_policy.md) for details.

### How do I return a book?
1. Contact customer support within 30 days
2. Provide your order number
3. We'll assess eligibility and provide a return label (if eligible)
4. Ship the book back
5. Refund processed within 5-7 days of receiving the return

### Can I return e-books or audiobooks?
Digital products are generally non-returnable once downloaded. Exceptions may be made for technical issues or accidental purchases reported within 24 hours.

### How long do refunds take?
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your return. It may take an additional 3-5 business days for your bank to post the credit.

### Can I exchange a book for a different title?
We don't offer direct exchanges. Please return the original book for a refund and place a new order for the title you want.

## 🌟 Special Circumstances

### What if I received a damaged book?
Contact us immediately with photos of the damage. We'll send a replacement at no cost with a prepaid return label for the damaged copy.

### What if I received the wrong book?
We apologize! Contact support right away. We'll ship the correct book immediately with a prepaid return label for the incorrect item.

### Can I return a book I already read?
No, our return policy requires books to be in unread, resellable condition. Books with bent spines, markings, or obvious signs of reading cannot be returned.

### What if a book is missing pages or has printing errors?
This is a manufacturer defect. Contact us for a free replacement. Keep the defective copy for return.

### Do you price match?
We strive to offer competitive prices but don't have a formal price matching policy. However, contact us if you find a significantly lower price elsewhere.

## 🔍 Technical Issues

### The website isn't loading properly
Try these steps:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
2. Try a different browser
3. Disable browser extensions
4. Check your internet connection
5. Contact support if issues persist: tech@bookly.com

### I can't download my e-book
Common solutions:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Ensure you're logged into the correct account
3. Try downloading on a different device
4. Verify your purchase in Order History
5. Contact support: digital@bookly.com

### The mobile app keeps crashing
1. Update to the latest app version
2. Restart your device
3. Clear app cache (in device settings)
4. Uninstall and reinstall the app
5. Contact support with your device model and OS version
