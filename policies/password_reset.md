# Bookly Password Reset & Account Recovery Guide

## How to Reset Your Password

### Method 1: Using the "Forgot Password" Link (Recommended)

1. **Go to the Login Page**
   - Visit www.bookly.com/login
   - Or open the Bookly mobile app and tap "Sign In"

2. **Click "Forgot Password?"**
   - Link located below the password field
   - You'll be redirected to the password reset page

3. **Enter Your Email Address**
   - Use the email associated with your Bookly account
   - Click "Send Reset Link"

4. **Check Your Email**
   - You'll receive an email within 2-5 minutes
   - Subject line: "Bookly Password Reset Request"
   - **Check spam folder** if you don't see it

5. **Click the Reset Link**
   - Link is valid for **2 hours**
   - Opens a secure password reset page

6. **Create New Password**
   - Enter your new password (see requirements below)
   - Confirm the new password
   - Click "Reset Password"

7. **Success!**
   - You'll see a confirmation message
   - Log in with your new password

### Method 2: Through Your Account Settings (If Already Logged In)

1. Log in to your Bookly account
2. Go to **Account Settings** → **Security**
3. Click **"Change Password"**
4. Enter your current password
5. Enter and confirm your new password
6. Click **"Update Password"**

## Password Requirements

Your new password must meet these requirements:
- ✅ **Minimum 8 characters** (12+ recommended)
- ✅ **At least one uppercase letter** (A-Z)
- ✅ **At least one lowercase letter** (a-z)
- ✅ **At least one number** (0-9)
- ✅ **At least one special character** (@, #, $, %, etc.)
- ❌ **Cannot be a previously used password**
- ❌ **Cannot contain your name or email address**

### Examples of Strong Passwords
- `ReadBooks2026!`
- `MyFav0rite$Story`
- `B00kW0rm#Life`

### Password Strength Tips
- Use a passphrase: `Coffee&Books@Morning!`
- Mix words and numbers: `3BlueCats#Reading`
- Use a password manager for complex passwords

## Troubleshooting

### I Didn't Receive the Reset Email

**Wait a Few Minutes**
- Delivery can take up to 5 minutes
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Check "Promotions" or "Updates" tabs in Gmail

**Verify Your Email Address**
- Make sure you're using the correct email
- Check for typos in the email field
- Try any other email addresses you may have used

**Resend the Email**
- Go back to the forgot password page
- Click "Resend Email"
- Wait 5 minutes before requesting again

**Still Not Receiving It?**
- Add `noreply@bookly.com` to your contacts
- Check with your email provider about blocking
- Contact support: support@bookly.com

### The Reset Link Expired

**Links expire after 2 hours for security**
- Request a new reset link
- Complete the process within 2 hours
- Only the most recent link is valid

### I Don't Remember My Email Address

**If you know your username:**
1. Contact support with your username
2. We'll verify your identity
3. Send reset link to email on file

**If you have order history:**
1. Find any order confirmation email
2. The "to" address is your account email
3. Use that email to reset password

**If you don't have access to anything:**
1. Contact support: 1-800-BOOKLY1
2. Provide identifying information:
   - Full name
   - Shipping address
   - Recent order numbers
   - Last 4 digits of payment method
3. We'll verify your identity and help recover account

### My New Password Isn't Working

**Common Issues:**
- **Caps Lock is on** (passwords are case-sensitive)
- **Extra spaces** before or after the password
- **Autocorrect changed characters** (especially on mobile)
- **Using old password** (wait 5 minutes for system update)

**Solutions:**
- Try typing password in a text editor first, then copy-paste
- Reset password again with a completely different password
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser or incognito mode

### Account Locked After Too Many Failed Attempts

**For Security, accounts lock after 5 failed login attempts**

**Automatic Unlock:**
- Account automatically unlocks after **30 minutes**

**Immediate Unlock:**
1. Use the "Forgot Password" link to reset
2. Reset link will unlock your account
3. Create a new password

**Still Locked?**
- Contact support: support@bookly.com
- We'll verify your identity and unlock immediately

## Account Security Best Practices

### Protecting Your Account

**Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**
- Go to Account Settings → Security
- Enable 2FA for extra protection
- Use authenticator app or SMS

**Use a Unique Password**
- Don't reuse passwords from other sites
- Change password every 6-12 months
- Use a password manager

**Watch for Suspicious Activity**
- Review login history in Account Settings
- Check for unfamiliar orders
- Monitor email for unauthorized changes

**Log Out on Shared Devices**
- Always log out on public computers
- Don't save passwords in shared browsers
- Clear browser history after use

### Recognizing Phishing Attempts

**Bookly will NEVER:**
- ❌ Ask for your password via email
- ❌ Request full credit card numbers
- ❌ Threaten account closure without notice
- ❌ Ask you to "verify" account info via email link

**Legitimate Bookly Emails Come From:**
- ✅ @bookly.com domain only
- ✅ noreply@bookly.com (automated emails)
- ✅ support@bookly.com (customer service)

**If You Receive Suspicious Email:**
1. Do NOT click any links
2. Do NOT provide any information
3. Forward to: security@bookly.com
4. Delete the email

## Changing Your Email Address

**To update your account email:**

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to **Account Settings** → **Email & Notifications**
3. Click **"Change Email Address"**
4. Enter your current password for verification
5. Enter your new email address
6. Click **"Send Verification Email"**
7. Check your NEW email for verification link
8. Click the link to confirm the change

**Important Notes:**
- You'll need to verify your new email before it takes effect
- Order confirmations will go to the new email
- Your old email will receive a notification of the change

## Additional Account Recovery Options

### If You Can't Access Your Email Account

**Temporary Email Access Issues:**
- Wait until you regain email access
- Contact your email provider for help

**Permanent Email Loss:**
1. Contact Bookly support: 1-800-BOOKLY1
2. Provide proof of identity:
   - Government-issued ID
   - Recent order confirmation
   - Billing address and payment method info
3. We'll help you update your email address

### Suspicious Account Activity

1. **Immediately change your password**
2. **Check order history** for unauthorized purchases
3. **Review account settings** for changes
4. **Enable two-factor authentication**
5. **Contact support immediately:** security@bookly.com

**We'll help you:**
- Secure your account
- Investigate unauthorized activity
- Reverse fraudulent charges
- Set up enhanced security
