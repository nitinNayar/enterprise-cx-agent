# Bookly Privacy Policy (Last Updated: FEB 2026)

## Introduction

At Bookly, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our mobile application, or make a purchase from us.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, password, phone number
- **Shipping Information:** Delivery address, billing address
- **Payment Information:** Credit card details, payment method (processed securely)
- **Profile Information:** Reading preferences, favorite genres, book reviews
- **Communication:** Messages sent through customer support, chat, or email

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information:** IP address, browser type, operating system
- **Usage Data:** Pages viewed, time spent, click patterns, search queries
- **Cookies:** Session cookies, preference cookies, analytics cookies
- **Location Data:** General geographic location (city/state level)

### Information from Third Parties
- **Social Media:** If you connect social media accounts
- **Payment Processors:** Transaction confirmation from Stripe, PayPal, etc.
- **Fraud Prevention:** Information from fraud detection services

## How We Use Your Information

### To Provide Our Services
- Process and fulfill your orders
- Send order confirmations and shipping notifications
- Manage your account and preferences
- Provide customer support
- Process returns and refunds

### To Improve Our Services
- Analyze user behavior and preferences
- Personalize book recommendations
- Improve website functionality and user experience
- Develop new features and services
- Conduct market research

### To Communicate With You
- Send transactional emails (order updates, account changes)
- Send promotional emails (with your consent)
- Notify you of special offers and new releases
- Request feedback and reviews
- Send Book Club newsletters (if subscribed)

### For Security and Legal Compliance
- Prevent fraud and unauthorized access
- Comply with legal obligations
- Enforce our terms of service
- Protect our rights and property
- Resolve disputes

## How We Share Your Information

### We DO Share With
- **Shipping Carriers:** To deliver your orders (USPS, UPS, FedEx)
- **Payment Processors:** To process transactions securely (Stripe, PayPal)
- **Service Providers:** Email services, analytics providers, hosting providers
- **Book Publishers:** For direct fulfillment of special orders (with your consent)
- **Legal Authorities:** When required by law or to protect rights

### We DO NOT Share With
- **Third-party marketers** (we never sell your email list)
- **Data brokers**
- **Social media platforms** (unless you explicitly connect accounts)
- **Anyone else** without your explicit consent

## Your Rights and Choices

### Account Management
- **Access Your Data:** View all personal information we have on file
- **Update Information:** Change account details, shipping addresses, preferences
- **Delete Account:** Request complete account deletion
- **Export Data:** Download your data in portable format

### Communication Preferences
- **Unsubscribe:** Opt out of marketing emails (link in every email)
- **Notification Settings:** Control which emails you receive
- **SMS Opt-out:** Reply STOP to any text message
- **Phone Preferences:** Add number to do-not-call list

### Cookie Preferences
- **Cookie Consent:** Manage cookie preferences in account settings
- **Browser Settings:** Block cookies through browser settings
- **Analytics Opt-out:** Opt out of Google Analytics tracking

### California Privacy Rights (CCPA)
California residents have additional rights:
- **Right to Know:** What data we collect and how we use it
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal information
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Opt out of data sales (note: we don't sell data)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** Equal service regardless of privacy choices

### European Privacy Rights (GDPR)
EU/UK residents have additional rights:
- **Right to Access:** Obtain copy of your data
- **Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure:** Request data deletion ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to Portability:** Receive data in machine-readable format
- **Right to Object:** Object to certain data processing
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we use your data

## Data Security

### Security Measures
- **Encryption:** SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
- **Payment Security:** PCI DSS compliant payment processing
- **Access Controls:** Strict employee access limitations
- **Regular Audits:** Security assessments and penetration testing
- **Incident Response:** 24/7 monitoring and rapid response procedures

### What We Do
- Encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest
- Use secure servers and databases
- Regularly update and patch systems
- Train employees on data protection
- Implement multi-factor authentication

### What You Should Do
- Use strong, unique passwords
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Keep your account information up to date
- Log out of shared devices
- Report suspicious activity immediately

## Data Retention

### How Long We Keep Data
- **Account Information:** Until account deletion + 30 days
- **Order History:** 7 years (for tax and legal compliance)
- **Payment Information:** Not stored (tokenized by payment processor)
- **Support Tickets:** 3 years for quality assurance
- **Marketing Data:** Until you unsubscribe + 60 days
- **Analytics Data:** Aggregated and anonymized after 26 months

## Children's Privacy

- **Age Requirement:** Must be 13+ to create an account
- **Parental Consent:** Required for users under 18
- **Data Collection:** We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13
- **Violation Reporting:** Contact us if you believe a child has created an account

## Cookies and Tracking

### Types of Cookies We Use
- **Essential Cookies:** Required for site functionality (shopping cart, login)
- **Preference Cookies:** Remember your settings and preferences
- **Analytics Cookies:** Help us understand how you use the site
- **Marketing Cookies:** Used to show relevant ads (with your consent)

### Third-Party Cookies
- **Google Analytics:** Website traffic analysis
- **Social Media Pixels:** If you engage with social features
- **Advertising Networks:** For retargeting (only with consent)

### Managing Cookies
- Adjust preferences in your account settings
- Use browser settings to block cookies
- Note: Blocking essential cookies may limit functionality
