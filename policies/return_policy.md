# Bookly Return Policy (Last Updated: FEB 2026)

## CRITICAL ENFORCEMENT RULES
**You must Cross-Reference EVERY item against this list.**
**IF an item falls into these categories, you must DENY the return, even if the System says "Eligible: True".**

## 1. Non-Returnable Categories (FINAL SALE)
* **Digital Goods:** E-books, Audiobooks (downloaded), Digital Gift Cards. -> **ACTION: REJECT**
* **Personalized Items:** Books with custom inscriptions, personalized bookplates. -> **ACTION: REJECT**
* **Used/Read Books:** Books with visible signs of reading (bent pages, markings, wear). -> **ACTION: REJECT**
* **Damaged by Customer:** Books damaged by customer (water damage, torn pages, stains). -> **ACTION: REJECT**

## 2. Standard Eligibility
* Physical books (hardcover, paperback) are eligible for return within 30 days of delivery.
* Books must be in **unread, resellable condition** (no bent spines, markings, or damage).

## 1. General Eligibility
Books are generally eligible for return within 30 days of delivery if they meet condition requirements.

## 2. Category Specific Rules

### Physical Books (Hardcover, Paperback, Collectibles)
- Must be in **unread, resellable condition**.
- No bent spines, dog-eared pages, highlighting, or annotations.
- Original packaging (if shrink-wrapped) must be intact.
- IF the customer mentions they have **read, opened, or started** the book, the return must be **DENIED**.
- Exception: Books damaged in shipping are always returnable with photos.

### Digital Products (E-books, Audiobooks)
- **NON-RETURNABLE** once downloaded or accessed.
- NO EXCEPTIONS, even if customer hasn't read/listened yet.
- Technical issues (can't download, wrong format) should be handled by Tech Support, not Returns.
- Accidental purchases must be reported within 24 hours for consideration.

### Signed & Limited Editions
- Must be in **pristine, unopened condition** if shrink-wrapped.
- IF the customer has opened or examined the book, the return is **DENIED** (collectible value lost).
- Personalized signatures/inscriptions requested at purchase are **FINAL SALE** (non-returnable).

### Book Accessories (Bookmarks, Book Lights, Bookends, etc.)
- Standard 30-day return policy.
- Must be unused and in original packaging.

### Gift Cards (Physical & Digital)
- All **Gift Cards** are **NON-REFUNDABLE** once delivered.
- NO EXCEPTIONS, even if the card has not been redeemed.

### Wrong Item Shipped or Damaged in Transit
- **ALWAYS RETURNABLE** regardless of other policy rules.
- Customer should provide photos of damage or wrong item.
- Full refund or replacement offered immediately.

## 3. Book Condition Guidelines

**What is "Unread Condition"?**
- Spine is straight and uncreased
- No page corners turned down (dog-earing)
- No writing, highlighting, or annotations
- No visible signs of handling beyond opening the package
- Dust jacket (if applicable) is intact

**What is NOT "Unread Condition"?**
- Cracked spine or visible creasing
- Bent or folded pages
- Any markings (pen, pencil, highlighter)
- Coffee stains, water damage, food residue
- Missing dust jacket or torn covers

## 4. Book Club Member Benefits
Book Club VIP members may receive extended consideration for returns in special circumstances. See VIP exception protocol for details.

## 5. Holiday Gift Policy
Books purchased in November-December may qualify for extended return windows for holiday gifts. Gift recipients should include original order information.

## 6. Override Rule
This document is the **FINAL AUTHORITY**. Even if the system flags an order as "Eligible", you must deny the return if it violates the category rules above.

## 7. Special Circumstances

### Book Club Selections
- Monthly book club selections follow standard policy.
- Subscription cancellations are separate from individual returns.

### Pre-Orders
- Pre-ordered books can be canceled before shipping.
- Once shipped, standard return policy applies.

### International Orders
- International returns follow the same 30-day policy but may take longer to process.
- Customers responsible for return shipping costs on international orders.

## 8. Return Shipping

**Who Pays for Return Shipping?**
- **Bookly's Error** (wrong item, damaged): We provide prepaid label
- **Customer Changed Mind**: Customer pays return shipping
- **Defective Item**: We provide prepaid label
- **VIP Members**: May receive free return shipping based on tier

## 9. Refund Timeline
- Refunds processed within 5-7 business days of receiving returned item
- Original payment method used
- E-book/audiobook refunds (if approved) are immediate
