# Bookly Shipping Policy (Last Updated: FEB 2026)

## Standard Shipping Options

### Domestic Shipping (United States)

#### Standard Shipping (5-7 Business Days)
- **Cost:** FREE on orders over $35, otherwise $4.99
- **Delivery:** 5-7 business days from order processing
- **Tracking:** Provided via email once shipped
- **Availability:** All physical books and accessories

#### Expedited Shipping (2-3 Business Days)
- **Cost:** $9.99 flat rate
- **Delivery:** 2-3 business days from order processing
- **Tracking:** Real-time tracking provided
- **Cutoff Time:** Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship same day

#### Express Shipping (1-2 Business Days)
- **Cost:** $19.99 flat rate
- **Delivery:** 1-2 business days from order processing
- **Tracking:** Real-time tracking with signature confirmation
- **Cutoff Time:** Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship same day
- **Weekend Delivery:** Available for additional $10

### International Shipping

#### International Standard (10-21 Business Days)
- **Cost:** Starting at $14.99 (varies by destination)
- **Delivery:** 10-21 business days
- **Customs:** Customer responsible for customs fees and duties
- **Tracking:** International tracking provided
- **Restrictions:** Some items may not be available for international shipping

#### International Expedited (5-10 Business Days)
- **Cost:** Starting at $34.99 (varies by destination)
- **Delivery:** 5-10 business days
- **Customs:** Customer responsible for customs fees and duties
- **Tracking:** Full international tracking with signature

## Order Processing Time

- **Standard Orders:** Processed within 1-2 business days
- **Pre-Orders:** Ship on release date
- **Custom Orders:** Processing time varies (notification provided at checkout)
- **Book Club Subscriptions:** Ship on the 1st of each month

## Shipping Restrictions

### Items Not Available for Shipping
- Rare collectibles may have shipping restrictions
- Some international destinations have import restrictions on books
- Hazardous materials (if applicable to accessories) cannot be shipped via air

### Address Restrictions
- We ship to all 50 US states, including Alaska and Hawaii
- PO Boxes accepted for standard shipping
- APO/FPO addresses supported (use military shipping rates)
- Cannot ship to forwarding services for international orders

## Tracking Your Order

### How to Track
1. Check your email for shipping confirmation
2. Click the tracking link provided
3. Or log in to your Bookly account and view "My Orders"
4. Track packages on carrier website (USPS, UPS, FedEx)

### Expected Tracking Updates
- **Order Confirmed:** Within 1 hour of purchase
- **Processing:** 1-2 business days
- **Shipped:** Tracking number activated
- **In Transit:** Regular updates from carrier
- **Out for Delivery:** Day of delivery notification
- **Delivered:** Confirmation with delivery time

## Shipping Issues

### Lost or Stolen Packages
- **Responsibility:** Bookly is responsible until delivery confirmation
- **Action:** Contact support if package shows delivered but not received
- **Investigation:** We will work with carrier to locate package
- **Resolution:** Replacement or refund provided if package cannot be located
- **Timeframe:** Allow 5-7 business days for investigation

### Damaged in Transit
- **Report Immediately:** Contact support within 48 hours of delivery
- **Photo Required:** Please provide photos of damage
- **Resolution:** Replacement shipped immediately at no cost
- **Return:** Keep damaged item until replacement arrives (for carrier claim)

### Wrong Item Shipped
- **Report:** Contact support immediately
- **Resolution:** Correct item shipped immediately with prepaid return label
- **Cost:** No charge to customer for our error

### Delayed Shipments
- **Weather/Carrier Delays:** Beyond our control, but we'll assist in tracking
- **Processing Delays:** We'll notify you if order is delayed
- **Compensation:** Extended delivery times may qualify for shipping refund
